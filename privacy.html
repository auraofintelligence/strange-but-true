Privacy

Plain privacy for digital help and downloads.

How Strange but True handles information shared for practical help, enquiries and music orders.

Client devices

I only look at what is needed to help with the task in front of us. I do not copy, store or share personal files unless you clearly ask me to as part of the work.

Please do not send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases or one-time codes through website forms or messages.

Downloads and checkout

Music orders collect your name, delivery email, album selection and payment reference. These details are recorded in a private order sheet hosted by Google and used to fulfil your order, provide support and keep business records. Stripe or PayPal processes the payment on its own checkout page; card details are not collected by this website. After a verified payment, an order notification is sent to Luke for manual delivery.
