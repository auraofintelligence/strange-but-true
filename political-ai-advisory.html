AI Advisory for Public Leaders

Understand the tools. Lead with purpose.

Practical AI advisory for elected politicians, public portfolio holders, councils, mayors and executive leaders navigating decisions that affect people and communities.

Discuss an engagement

What we can work on

  • Briefings on AI capabilities, limits and emerging choices
  • Research, evidence trails and questions for public decisions
  • Office workflows, document systems and human-reviewed drafts
  • Workshops, use-case planning and practical prototypes
  • Longer-term questions about public institutions, communities and powerful intelligence

Begin with your priorities

Bring a decision, portfolio, community challenge or opportunity. We can agree on the questions, the work and the people who need to review it, then choose a useful first step.

Briefings and prototypes support human judgement. Source material, information handling and delivery arrangements are agreed for each engagement.

Research and public submissions

Built on a wider body of work.

Luke's advisory work grows from public submissions, research and proposals spanning local government funding, AI and data infrastructure, and Australia's regional relationships. This work connects local communities with state, national and international questions.

From public policy to practical proposals

Installing the Missing Middle develops a proposal for community compute and local ownership. Escape Room Team Building explores an Australian network connecting learning, tourism, paid work and community infrastructure. Both draw on the broader research and public-policy work.
