Paid community contributions

Paid work and voluntary hours, side by side.

The proposed iCi/SOL/XRP pathway is an avenue for recording paid work connected to community projects and recognising paid community contributions through a local leaderboard. The details of the public record and leaderboard are still being developed.

C-Hours sit alongside this idea as a separate record of freely given human time. One verified C-Hour recognises one voluntary human hour; it has no monetary conversion. Paid work and its leaderboard are not part of C-Hours.

People can explore project ideas, discuss what matters and choose how they would like to take part. Public recognition and wallet-based records are proposed options, not requirements for ordinary enquiries or services.
