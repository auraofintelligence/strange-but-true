Strange but True is not currently registered for GST, so no GST is charged. Payment methods and timing are agreed before work begins. Approved local and organisational customers may use 7-14 day invoices; first-time, urgent or unclear jobs may need a deposit or upfront payment.

Lower island rates are a deliberate local choice, not a sign that the work is low-value. Discounts and favours are not automatic entitlements. They are offered at Luke's discretion for kind, respectful people and proactive, responsible community situations, not because someone hits an age marker, knows the right person, or arrives through a referral.
